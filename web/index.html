Sources, coverage & privacy

Weather and forecasts: Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) City Page Weather, currently an experimental API. AQHI: ECCC observations and forecast periods. Alert geometry and wording: ECCC weather alerts. All source times remain visible; fetched time does not replace an observation or forecast issue time.

A nearby station may be far from your place. We show its distance and hide a suggested AQHI reading beyond the configured distance limit. Quebec AQHI observation coverage is unavailable in this federal feed. Missing values remain unknown.

Choosing a place sends its weather ID and coordinates to api.weather.gc.ca. The map requests visible tiles from OpenStreetMap. Location permission is requested only after pressing Use my location. Saved places and limits remain in this browser's local storage; Clear saved places removes those places. No API key or account is required. The shared CoolCode counter records aggregate page opens.
